# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

EduPilot is a project for people who want to make learning with AI more useful and accessible. We welcome participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

We are committed to making project spaces welcoming, respectful, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions in good faith.
- Give feedback about the work, not personal attributes.
- Welcome questions from first-time contributors.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, private study materials, or credentials.
- Accept that maintainers may set scope and prioritize work based on project capacity.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, or private learning records.
- Spam, deceptive promotion, or requests for artificial Stars and engagement.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior is inappropriate.

To report a conduct concern, contact **qiuyanzihan2004@gmail.com**. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.

This policy applies to project Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the EduPilot project.
