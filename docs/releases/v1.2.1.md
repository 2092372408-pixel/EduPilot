## 中文

EduPilot v1.2.1 改进教材问答的证据核验与回答展示。

- 涉及教材内容的回答在完成证据核验后再显示，等待期间可能需要更长时间。
- 核验会检查教材证据是否支持回答；证据不足时提示资料不足，减少无依据的教材内容和来源引用。
- 问候和明确的课外闲聊继续逐步显示回答；可能使用教材的其他知识问答需等本轮核验结束。

这些改进只能降低模型错误的概率，不能杜绝错误，也不能保证教材解析和检索始终完整、准确。请对照原文核对知识点、页码及引用；资料不足时可补充相关教材页面或说明。

下载适合系统架构的安装包，使用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 核对。安装包未签名，macOS 包未公证，系统可能提示或阻止打开。更新前请完成当前任务并完全退出应用；原生应用更新保留同一系统用户的配置与学习数据。旧浏览器脚本版数据不会自动迁入，请保留原目录备份。

## English

EduPilot v1.2.1 improves evidence checks and answer display for textbook questions.

- Answers involving textbook content appear after evidence checks finish. This may increase the wait before an answer is shown.
- Checks assess whether textbook evidence supports the answer. When evidence is insufficient, the app indicates that more material is needed, reducing unsupported textbook claims and source references.
- Greetings and clearly off-topic conversation continue to stream; other knowledge questions that may use the textbook wait for verification.

These changes can only reduce the likelihood of model errors; they cannot eliminate errors or guarantee complete, accurate textbook parsing and retrieval. Check explanations, page numbers and citations against the original material. Add relevant textbook pages or clarification when evidence is missing.

Download the package for your operating system and architecture and verify it with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Installers are unsigned and macOS packages are not notarized; your OS may warn or block opening them. Finish active tasks and quit before updating. Native updates retain the same system user's settings and study records; older browser-script data is not migrated automatically, so keep its original directory as a backup.
