# EduPilot v2.1.0

## 中文

把教材变成课程，用练习检验理解，把进步积累成自己的学习成果。v2.1 新增个人学习主页，将课程进度、技能领域和成果分享放在一起。

### 新增功能

- **个人学习主页**：集中查看各课程的学习进度、已学知识点和复习通过记录，一键回到课程继续学习。
- **技能领域**：按课程与章节整理正在学习的领域，查看知识点的学习与验证状态，并连接到个人知识图谱。
- **分享学习成果**：选择要展示的课程，预览成果卡，保存为 PNG 图片或使用系统分享。分享卡附项目地址与二维码，方便朋友一起体验 EduPilot。
- **更直接的项目介绍**：中英文首页突出从教材、练习到持续积累的学习流程，下载和上手入口更集中。

## English

Turn your materials into courses, test your understanding through practice, and build a record of your progress. v2.1 brings courses, skill areas and shareable learning achievements together in a personal learning home.

### What's new

- **Personal learning home**: see progress across your courses, studied concepts and passed review checks, then continue learning with one click.
- **Skill areas**: explore what you are learning by course and chapter, see concept progress and review results, and open your personal knowledge map.
- **Share your progress**: choose courses, preview a learning card, and save a PNG image or use system sharing. Each card includes the project link and a QR code so friends can try EduPilot.
- **A clearer introduction**: the Chinese and English project pages focus on the journey from materials and practice to lasting learning records, with direct download and getting-started links.
