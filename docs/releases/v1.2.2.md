## 中文

- 在对话输入框左下角切换「深度思考」。浏览器按模型记住选择，下一条消息生效。开关是否可用取决于模型和接入平台。
- 模型设置新增各平台的官方接口文档入口。连接测试和对话使用统一的请求配置，按平台和模型发送对应参数。
- OpenRouter 的 Qwen 3.5 Flash（`qwen/qwen3.5-flash-02-23`）固定关闭深度思考，避免该接口把思考过程混进回复。模型因地区限制不可用时会提示换用其他模型。

请下载对应系统和架构的安装包，并使用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 核对。安装包未签名，macOS 包未公证，系统可能提示或阻止打开。更新前请先完全退出应用。桌面版使用同一系统用户的数据目录。

## English

- Toggle Deep thinking at the lower left of the chat composer. Your browser remembers the choice per model and applies it to your next message. Availability depends on the model and provider.
- Model settings link to official provider API documentation. Connection checks and chat share request settings for each provider and model.
- Deep thinking stays off for OpenRouter's Qwen 3.5 Flash (`qwen/qwen3.5-flash-02-23`) to keep reasoning traces out of replies. When a model is unavailable in your region, the app prompts you to choose another one.

Download the package for your operating system and architecture and verify it with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Installers are unsigned and macOS packages are not notarized. Your operating system may warn or block opening them. Quit the app before updating. The desktop app uses the same data directory for the same operating system user.
