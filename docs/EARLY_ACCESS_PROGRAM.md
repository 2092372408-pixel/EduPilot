# EduPilot Early Access Program

EduPilot is preparing its first community-led Early Access cohort. The initial goal is to welcome around 100 people who are interested in improving a practical, end-to-end AI learning workspace.

This page describes the proposed program. Recruitment will begin only after the maintainer confirms the public launch details and reward budget.

## Who we are looking for

- Learners who want to study from their own materials and track progress over time.
- Developers interested in AI agents, RAG, learning tools, knowledge management, or local-first applications.
- Open-source contributors who can help improve documentation, testing, accessibility, reliability, or code.
- Researchers and educators interested in evaluating AI-supported learning workflows.

## Ways to participate

1. **Product tester** — install EduPilot, try a real learning workflow, and share actionable feedback.
2. **Deep tester** — test multiple workflows or platforms, provide reproducible bug reports, and help verify fixes.
3. **Community contributor** — improve documentation, tests, issue triage, design, or code through an agreed contribution process.

No contribution is required to leave an honest review. Positive feedback is not expected or rewarded.

## Thank-you model credits

Eligible participants may receive **RMB 5–100 worth of model credits** for meaningful, verifiable participation. Credits are intended for supported model services such as Qwen or DeepSeek.

Indicative contribution bands:

| Contribution | Indicative credit value |
| --- | ---: |
| Completed first-run experience and useful structured feedback | RMB 5–15 |
| Reproducible bug report, multi-workflow test, or actionable product review | RMB 15–50 |
| Substantial documentation, test, design, or code contribution; sustained community support | RMB 50–100 |

The final amount is determined by the maintainer based on usefulness, effort, and verification. These are model credits, not cash. Availability, provider, delivery method, and any applicable limits will be confirmed before an award is issued. Credits are not guaranteed merely for signing up, starring, following, forking, or posting praise.

## How to take part

1. Read the [README](../README.md) and [Beta Testing Guide](BETA_TESTING_GUIDE.md).
2. Download the latest public release from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/2092372408-pixel/EduPilot/releases/latest).
3. Try one or more workflows using your own non-sensitive materials.
4. Share feedback through the [Beta Feedback issue form](https://github.com/2092372408-pixel/EduPilot/issues/new?template=beta_feedback.yml), or contribute through the repository's documented process.
5. If you would like to be considered for model credits, contact the maintainer privately after submitting your feedback. Do not publish email addresses, account details, API keys, or payment information in public issues.

## What useful feedback looks like

- What operating system and EduPilot version did you use?
- What were you trying to accomplish?
- What steps did you take?
- What worked well, and where did you get stuck?
- What did you expect to happen, and what happened instead?
- If you suggest a change, what learner problem would it solve?

Specific, reproducible observations are more useful than star counts or generic praise.

## Privacy and safety

- Use sample or non-sensitive learning materials when possible.
- Never post API keys, access tokens, private documents, personal learning records, or private contact information in public issues.
- AI features send relevant prompts, excerpts, or attachments to the model provider configured by the user. Review that provider's privacy terms before use.
- Participation is voluntary. You can stop testing at any time.
- Public feedback may be visible to anyone. Share only information you are comfortable making public.
